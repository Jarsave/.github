# JarSave

**Kids learn money by living it.**

An app for iPhone and Android where kids earn money from real effort, save it, and spend it on wishes their parents approve.

- **Earn:** practice time, certificates and report cards earn money from the family's monthly budget.
- **Save:** money left in the jar earns monthly interest, at a rate the parent sets.
- **Spend:** a kid photographs or says a wish; the parent prices it and approves it.
- **Lifetimeline:** every paper and practice hour, kept as a real record that belongs to the kid at 18.

No ads, no data sold. Waitlist open at [jarsave.com](https://jarsave.com).
